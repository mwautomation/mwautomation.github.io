Privacy policy

This notice covers the MW AI Solutions website and enquiries sent through its contact form. Updated: 9 November 2026.

Controller and contact

The data controller is Michał Włodarczyk — MW AI Solutions, Piotrków Trybunalski, Poland. NIP: 7712937149; REGON: 541254322.

For personal-data requests, use the contact form. Describe your request in the message; the email address you provide will be used to reply.

Information and purposes

The form sends your full name, email address and message. These details are used to answer your enquiry, arrange a conversation and discuss potential cooperation. Providing them is voluntary but necessary to submit the form and receive a reply. Please do not send sensitive information or unnecessary information about other people.

When you enquire about entering a contract on your own behalf, processing is based on steps taken at your request before entering that contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Other correspondence, including contact on behalf of a company, relies on the controller’s legitimate interests in handling enquiries and business communication (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Keeping the website available and secure also serves those interests.

External services and recipients

  • Formspree, Inc. handles form submissions and forwards messages to the controller. During submission it may also process technical information such as your IP address and browser details. See the Formspree privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Page requests send its provider technical information needed to deliver the site, including your IP address. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies typefaces from Google’s servers. Downloading them connects your browser to Google and sends your IP address and request information. See the Google privacy policy.
  • Correspondence may also be handled by providers delivering and storing messages. Competent authorities may receive information when required by law.

External links, such as LinkedIn and Grok Bot, lead to separate services whose privacy terms apply when you visit them.

Processing outside the EEA

Providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Formspree states that it processes information in the United States and other countries where it operates; GitHub and Google also use international infrastructure. The linked provider policies describe their locations and protection mechanisms. You can request information about the processing of details submitted through this form by contacting the controller through the form.

Retention

Correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and related discussions. If cooperation follows, retention depends on performing the contract and applicable legal obligations. Information needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims may be retained until the relevant limitation periods expire. It is deleted when the purpose and applicable grounds for keeping it no longer apply. Provider policies describe retention for information they process for their own purposes.

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR’s conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion and restriction of processing. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Data portability applies where its legal conditions are met, particularly for automated processing based on a contract or consent.

Use the contact form to make a request. You may also complain to the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). Form submissions are not used for solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects, or profiling for that purpose.

Analytics and cookies

The website contains no first-party analytics scripts, advertising pixels or code that sets cookies. Enquiries are not added to marketing lists. External services still receive the technical information described above; their separate websites may use their own technologies under their respective policies.
